Research Interests

I am interested in transportation system operation and management, especially data-driven approaches for improving road network efficiency under constrained transportation resources.

Traffic Flow Management

Traffic state identification, congestion management, active traffic control, and refined management strategies for highway and arterial systems.

Differential Tolling

Highway pricing strategies, route choice behavior, traffic redistribution, and network efficiency improvement through differentiated toll policies.

Transportation Data Analysis

Large-scale tolling and OD data processing, traffic pattern identification, and empirical analysis of transportation policies.
